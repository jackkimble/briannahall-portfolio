# Editing your website

Everything on your site can be changed from **app.pagescms.org**. Sign in with the email
invitation link, then open the site. The menu on the left lists each part of the site.
When you click **Save**, the live site updates about a minute later.

If a change doesn't show up, your browser may be showing a saved copy of the page (it can keep
one for up to 10 minutes). Press **Ctrl + Shift + R** (Windows) or **Cmd + Shift + R** (Mac) to
load the newest version.

## Add photos to an album
1. **Photo albums** → pick the album.
2. Under **Photos**, click the add button, then upload or pick a photo.
3. Type a short **Description** (for example “Driver waving from car 258”). It helps people
   using screen readers and helps Google find your work.
4. Drag entries up or down to change the order. **Save**.

Photos straight off your camera or phone are fine. The site makes smaller copies itself.
Tick **Show this photo big, on its own row** for your favorites; the rest arrange themselves.

## Start a new album
**Photo albums** → add a new entry. Give it a name, a cover photo and a **Position** number
(1 shows first). Add photos as above. It appears on the Photography page automatically.

## Add a brand project
**Brand projects** → add a new entry. Fill in the title, a card image and a position. Then add
**Sections**: each one is some writing with a photo or video beside it.
- **Layout** chooses which side the photo sits on, or full width under the writing.
- **Frame** wraps a photo or video in a phone or laptop, like your Instagram and guideline pages.
- For videos, add a **Video still** (a screenshot) and keep files under 20 MB.
- Turn on **Play the video silently on a loop** only for clips without sound.

## Merch and illustrations
**Merch designs** and **Illustrations & designs** each hold one list. Add, remove or reorder
entries the same way as album photos.

## Words on the home page
**Home page** holds your introduction, the blue quote, the four work tiles, your About text,
video and skills. In the About box, leave an empty line between paragraphs.

## Contact details
**Contact & site details** holds your email, phone, location and LinkedIn link. These appear
at the bottom of every page.

## If something looks wrong
Changes are saved as versions, so nothing is lost. Ask Jack to roll back to an earlier
version on GitHub.
